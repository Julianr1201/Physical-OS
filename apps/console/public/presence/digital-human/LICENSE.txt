female-executive.glb

The body, skin, hair, eyes, clothing, skeleton, and morph targets come from MakeHuman
(http://www.makehumancommunity.org). Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, and Jonas Hauquier
dedicated those assets to the public domain under CC0 1.0 in September 2020.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The file was exported by Ara 3D Gen (https://github.com/olatunde85/3d-gen), preset
"The Female Executive", without the preset sunglasses. The generator code is MIT,
copyright 2026 Josh Ola. Ara 3D Gen states that exported characters may be used in
commercial and non-commercial projects.

Motion clips are retargeted from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database
(http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu). CMU allows use in commercial products and forbids reselling
the motion data on its own. Acknowledgement: "The data used in this project was obtained
from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."

This export is not a finished digital human. It has no separate high, medium, and low
meshes, and several requested performances have no authored clip.
